Admission process

  1. B.Tech (KCC ITM, college code 492): based on JEE 2026 and 10+2 scores. Management seats are filled directly by the institute through counselling on JEE and 10+2 merit. Duration: 4 years (8 semesters).
  2. KCC ILHE (college code 274): qualify CUET (BBA, BCA, BJMC, B.Com) or CLAT (BA LLB (H), BBA LLB (H)). Minimum 50% in intermediate (5% relaxation for SC/ST/PwD).
  3. M.Tech: B.E./B.Tech with at least 60% aggregate (54% for SC/ST/PwD). GATE candidates are allotted first; remaining seats go to non-GATE candidates by qualifying-exam marks. Duration: 2 years (4 semesters).
  4. MBA: graduate in any stream with 50% (45% for reserved categories); provisional admission is possible for those awaiting results.

Management quota is 15% of total intake as per university norms; up to 5% of management seats are reserved for NRI candidates.
