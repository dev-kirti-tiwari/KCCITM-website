About Us

KCC Institutes · Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida

KCC Institute of Technology and Management (KCC ITM) is built on a sprawling 40-acre campus in Greater Noida. It is affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), formerly UPTU, approved by AICTE, and its Computer Science & Engineering programme is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). KCC ITM offers B.Tech and M.Tech programmes along with the MBA.

KCC Institutes celebrate a legacy of 22 years in education.

The campus is centrally air-conditioned and Wi-Fi enabled, with libraries, labs and resource centres, and a safe environment for students, especially women.

Vision

  • To transform ordinary aspirants into extraordinary professionals.
  • To have several campuses throughout India and overseas, removing the barrier of geography to a top-notch education.
  • To establish and maintain professional synergies for students’ successful careers.
  • To instil a strong sense of dedication and morality.
  • To introduce students to current industry norms and integrate theory and practice.

Mission

To provide students with a complete education that meets international standards, cultivate a culture of social responsibility, and gain recognition and acceptance as a world-class educational institution.

Quality objectives

  • Contribute to society by producing top-notch managers and engineers.
  • Continuously incorporate the latest ideas into the curriculum.
  • Run continuous improvement programmes for students and faculty.
  • Bring the latest technologies to campus and anticipate industry trends.
  • Encourage a research-centred approach to study.

KCC Group

Since its inception in 1951 the KCC Group, founded by Late Shri R.R. Agarwal and Late Shri G.P. Gupta, has grown into a comprehensive learning platform. KCC Institutes is an educational venture of KCC Software Limited.
