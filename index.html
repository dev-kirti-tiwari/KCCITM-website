About Us

KCC Institute of Technology and Management is built on a sprawling 40-acre campus in Greater Noida. It is affiliated to AKTU, approved by AICTE, and its Computer Science & Engineering programme is accredited by NBA. KCC ITM offers B.Tech, M.Tech and MBA programmes, alongside KCC ILHE (BBA, BCA, B.Com, BA(JMC), BA LLB, BBA LLB).

Part of the KCC Group, which has been in education since 1951.
